The power‑sketch for the layout‑ignoring design uses the wrong standard deviation. A design that builds one binary per arm and runs each many times has per‑run noise equal to the **within‑seed** standard deviation ( ~ 0.183 s, CV ≈ 1.50 %), not the pooled total standard deviation (0.217 s) that mixes between‑seed variation. Re‑computing with the correct within‑seed σ gives ≈ 36 runs/group, not ≈ 49 runs/group, and the claimed 6× factor is unsupported. More fundamentally, a single‑binary‑per‑arm design cannot separate the treatment effect from the random seed bias, so no amount of within‑group replication can reliably detect a treatment effect; the whole power comparison is therefore inapplicable.
